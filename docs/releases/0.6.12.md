# Keepresso Mobile 0.6.12 (preview)

- Recognize usable screens: an awake external monitor counts as normal desktop use, even with the lid closed. With every usable screen asleep, warn after one continuous awake minute. Keep one card as its wording changes between lid closed and screen off.
- Play Glass only at an observed physical lid closure without a usable awake screen. Display standby and an already-closed wake do not play it.
- Turn three-minute-old activity evidence into silent, dismissible history with the last confirmation time. It survives process loss and reboot; Android may delay the historical replacement after expiring the live card. Fresh normal/sleep evidence removes it; DarkWake cannot create or refresh it.
- Keep an unresolved Critical heat warning active through stopped brewing or lost contact. Use Android’s standard notification tone, repeated on the alarm audio stream. Ignore silences it for five minutes, including stale readings; confirmed lower Apple thermal state stops it.
- Offer optional Android Alarms & reminders access for precise Ignore timing. Without it, Android may delay rearming. Revoking that access can cancel a pending alarm; reopen the app to restore it with the fallback. Alarm volume zero, DND, notification/full-screen restrictions and force-stop remain system controls; no volume is forced.

Update both companions for screen-off semantics. Pairing and settings are preserved. Native Keepresso and the bundled upstream trigger engine are unchanged; the native wake/session evidence requirements from [0.6.10](0.6.10.md) still apply.

Investigated and implemented with an LLM. Automated verification includes the display/context matrix, alert timing, stale messages/callbacks, history/dismissal, thermal recovery, clock changes, reboot, process loss and Android notification behavior. Physical lid/monitor transitions and real system sleep remain owner-operated checks; no Critical heat was induced.
