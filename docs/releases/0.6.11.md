# Keepresso Mobile 0.6.11 (preview)

- Wait one continuous minute fully awake with the lid closed before showing the phone warning. Brief closed-lid wakes no longer create it.
- Keep Glass at the observed lid closure on the Mac. Waking with the lid already closed does not play Glass.
- Keep one ongoing phone card, with silent updates when brewing changes. Delivered sleep or lid-open evidence removes it immediately.
- Let Android expire the card after three minutes without fresh evidence, even if the app is no longer running. Reopening the app does not restore an expired card. Expiration does not establish that the Mac slept.

Pairing and settings are preserved. Native Keepresso is unchanged. The experimental native wake/session evidence requirements and platform compatibility described in [0.6.10](0.6.10.md) still apply.

This change was investigated and implemented with an LLM. Automated checks cover brief wakes, DarkWake exclusion, immediate closure sound cues, incident updates/resolution, and notification expiration without the app's timer. No real sleep or thermal alarm was triggered for verification.
